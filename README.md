# HDFC Bank

Research and analysis on HDFC Bank Ltd (NSE: HDFCBANK, BSE: 500180).

## Contents

- `annual-reports/` — Annual reports FY2012 to FY2026 (`AR<year>.pdf`), sourced from BSE via Screener.
- `concalls/` — Earnings-call transcripts and investor presentations, named `<year>-<month>_transcript.pdf` / `_presentation.pdf`, 2022 onwards.

## Known gaps

- Apr 2023 (Q4 FY23) earnings presentation and the Oct 2023 HDFC Bank Day deck are not on HDFC Bank's results page and are not included.
- Concall files from before 2022 are not available on Screener, apart from a May 2018 presentation that no longer resolves.
