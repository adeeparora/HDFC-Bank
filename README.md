# HDFC Bank

Research and analysis on HDFC Bank Ltd (NSE: HDFCBANK, BSE: 500180).

## Contents

- `annual-reports/` — Annual reports FY2012 to FY2026 (`AR<year>.pdf`), sourced from BSE via Screener.
- `concalls/` — Earnings-call transcripts and investor presentations, named `<year>-<month>_transcript.pdf` / `_presentation.pdf`, 2022 onwards.

## Known gaps

- Presentations hosted on hdfcbank.com for Jul 2023 to Oct 2025 (and the Oct 2023 HDFC Bank Day deck), plus the Jan 2024 and Oct 2023 (16th) transcripts hosted there, are not yet included.
- Apr 2025 presentation (NSE) not yet included.
- Concall files from before 2022 are not available on Screener except a May 2018 presentation that no longer resolves.
